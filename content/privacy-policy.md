## Introduction

We value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard information when you visit our website, including details about cookies, Google AdSense advertising, and compliance with applicable privacy regulations.

## Cookies

- **What are cookies?** Cookies are small text files stored on your device by your web browser that help us improve your browsing experience.
- **Types of cookies we use:**
  - **Essential cookies** – required for the website to function correctly (e.g., session management).
  - **Performance cookies** – collect anonymous data on how visitors interact with the site to help us improve performance and content.
  - **Targeting/Advertising cookies** – used to deliver personalized ads, including those served by Google AdSense.
- **Managing cookies** – You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings. Disabling essential cookies may affect site functionality.

## Google AdSense Advertising Disclosures

- **AdSense integration** – We use Google AdSense to display advertisements on our site. Google may place its own cookies and collect data such as IP address, device information, and browsing behavior to serve relevant ads.
- **Data sharing** – Google may share aggregated, non‑personally identifiable information with third parties for analytics and ad performance reporting.
- **Opt‑out options** – You can opt out of personalized Google ads by visiting the [Google Ads Settings](https://adssettings.google.com/) page or using the [Google Analytics Opt‑out Browser Add‑on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout).
- **Compliance** – Our use of AdSense complies with the Google AdSense Program Policies and applicable privacy laws, including the GDPR and CCPA.

## User Privacy Compliance

- **Data collection** – We collect only the data necessary to provide our services, such as email addresses for newsletter subscriptions or contact forms. All data is stored securely.
- **Legal bases** – Processing is based on legitimate interests, contract performance, or explicit consent where required (e.g., newsletter sign‑ups).
- **Your rights** – You have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict the processing of your personal data. To exercise these rights, contact us at privacy@example.com.
- **Data retention** – Personal data is retained only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purpose for which it was collected, unless a longer retention period is required by law.
- **Third‑party services** – In addition to Google AdSense, we may use other third‑party services (e.g., analytics, email marketing). Each provider has its own privacy policy, and we ensure they meet applicable privacy standards.

## Changes to This Policy
